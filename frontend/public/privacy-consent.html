Youth Data Privacy Consent & Full Disclosure Acknowledgment

Pursuant to Republic Act No. 10173 (Data Privacy Act of 2012), Republic Act No. 10742 (Sangguniang Kabataan Reform Act of 2015 as amended by RA 11768), and DILG Memorandum Circular No. 2023-068 (SK Full Public Disclosure Policy).

1. Purpose of Data Collection

The KABISIG system collects, processes, and stores your personal information for the following purposes:

  • Katipunan ng Kabataan (KK) Profiling: To establish and maintain the official youth database of your barangay in compliance with DILG MC 2022-033.
  • Youth Development Program Planning: To design, implement, and evaluate SK programs, projects, and activities based on youth demographic data.
  • Digital Youth ID Issuance: To generate a unique Digital Youth ID with a QR code for program registration and attendance tracking.
  • Compliance Reporting: To fulfill DILG, COA, and other government reporting requirements, including the verification of youth voter registration status for SK profiling.
  • Public Transparency: To publish aggregated, anonymized data on the SK Public Transparency Dashboard in compliance with DILG MC 2023-068.

2. Personal Information Collected

We collect the following personal information:

  • Full name (first, middle, last, suffix)
  • Birthdate, age, sex, civil status, and voter registration status
  • Residential address and zone/purok
  • Contact number and email address
  • Educational status, school, course, year level
  • Employment status and scholarship information
  • Government-issued ID (for verification)
  • Profile picture (optional)

3. Legal Basis for Processing

Your data is processed based on the following legal grounds:

  • Consent (Section 12(a) of RA 10173) — provided through this form
  • Legal obligation (Section 12(c)) — compliance with RA 10742 and DILG regulations
  • Public task (Section 12(e)) — fulfillment of SK's youth governance mandate

4. Data Privacy Rights

As a data subject under RA 10173, you have the right to:

  • Be informed — know how your data is collected and used
  • Object — withdraw consent at any time (subject to legal retention requirements)
  • Access — request a copy of your personal data
  • Rectify — correct inaccurate or erroneous entries
  • Erase or block — request deletion (subject to DILG retention rules)
  • Damages — claim compensation for unauthorized use
  • Data portability — obtain your data in a machine-readable format
  • Complain — file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission

5. Data Retention

Your personal data will be retained for the duration of your active KK membership (ages 15–30) and for a period of thereafter, in accordance with the applicable records-retention requirements. After this period, data will be securely archived or anonymized.

6. Data Sharing and Disclosure

Your data may be shared with:

  • The SK Chairperson and authorized SK Officials — for verification and program management
  • The SK Federation President — aggregated, anonymized analytics only (no individual youth records)
  • DILG, COA, and other government agencies — when legally required
  • The general public — aggregated, anonymized data on the Public Transparency Dashboard only

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for commercial purposes.

7. Consent Declaration

By signing below, I confirm that:

  • I have read and understood this Data Privacy Consent Form.
  • I voluntarily give my informed consent to the collection, processing, and storage of my personal information as described above.
  • I understand that I may withdraw this consent at any time by written notice to my Barangay SK Chairperson.
  • I affirm that all information I have provided is accurate, complete, and truthful.
  • I understand that any false information may result in the rejection of my registration or account suspension.
